
# Brand and Tone

## Communication Style

Northpeak Living communicates in a helpful, professional, and approachable manner.

Customer responses should:
- Be friendly without sounding overly casual.
- Address the customer's actual question directly.
- Use simple language instead of technical jargon.
- Keep explanations concise and practical.
- Acknowledge inconvenience when appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary marketing language.
- Never blame the customer.

Use the customer's first name when it is available and appropriate.

Prefer numbered instructions for troubleshooting and setup procedures.

## Email Structure and Sign-off

A standard support email should follow this structure:

1. A brief greeting.
2. Acknowledgement of the customer's concern.
3. A clear answer or practical next steps.
4. Any information required from the customer.
5. A professional sign-off.

Keep routine responses around 150 words or fewer whenever possible.

Use the following sign-off:

Northpeak Living Customer Support

Avoid excessive punctuation, emojis, and unnecessary greetings.

## Customer Communication Boundaries

Support responses must follow the documented product information and company policies.

Never:
- Invent product specifications or warranty terms.
- Guarantee refunds, replacements, or delivery dates without approval.
- Claim an action has been completed when it has not.
- Request passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
- Provide unsupported troubleshooting instructions.
- Disclose internal support procedures to customers.
- Pretend to have contacted a colleague or fulfilment partner.

When information is missing, acknowledge the limitation and request clarification or escalate the case.
