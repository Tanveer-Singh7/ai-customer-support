
# Shipping

## Order Processing and Delivery Estimates

Northpeak Living processes orders through its fulfilment partners.

Standard order processing generally takes 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.

Estimated delivery windows:

- Standard service: 3–6 business days after dispatch.
- Extended delivery regions: 5–9 business days after dispatch.

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.

Delivery estimates may change because of:
- Weather conditions.
- Transport disruptions.
- Regional service limitations.
- Incorrect delivery information.
- High order volumes.

Customers receive an order confirmation and tracking information when available.

## Tracking and Delivery Issues

Customers should use the tracking link provided in their dispatch notification to check delivery progress.

For general tracking questions:
- Explain how to access the tracking link.
- Clarify the difference between order processing and dispatch.
- Avoid interpreting an unchanged tracking status as proof that a parcel is lost.

For a parcel reported as delayed, lost, or incorrectly delivered, collect:
- Order number.
- Customer email address.
- Tracking reference, if available.
- Brief description of the issue.

Order-specific delivery investigations must be escalated to a support colleague.

Never invent tracking updates or promise a delivery date.

## Replacement Parts and Special Shipments

Approved replacement components are dispatched through the Northpeak Living fulfilment team.

General replacement-part handling:
- No additional shipping charge applies when the replacement is approved under an eligible warranty or fulfilment claim.
- Dispatch generally takes up to 2 business days after approval.
- Estimated transit time is 4–8 business days after dispatch.
- Tracking information is shared when the replacement shipment is registered.

Replacement shipments require confirmation of:
- Original order number.
- Product model.
- Required component reference.
- Confirmed delivery address.

The support team must approve replacement requests before dispatch.

Do not promise shipment confirmation or delivery dates.
