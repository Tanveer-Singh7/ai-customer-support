## Setup, Storage and Maintenance

For camping chairs:
1. Place the chair on a stable, level surface.
2. Open the frame completely.
3. Check that the locking joints are engaged.
4. Confirm the fabric is correctly positioned before sitting.

For folding tables:
1. Unfold the support frame.
2. Lock the frame mechanisms.
3. Attach or unfold the tabletop according to the supplied instructions.
4. Check that all legs are stable before placing items on the table.

Maintenance recommendations:
- Clean fabric with mild soap and a damp cloth.
- Allow products to dry completely before storage.
- Avoid prolonged exposure to moisture.
- Store products in their carry bags when not in use.
- Do not use damaged frames or locking mechanisms.

## Product Problems and Support Procedures

Follow these guidelines when handling outdoor-equipment complaints.

- Missing accessories: Verify the model and package contents before arranging a replacement.
- Damaged fabric: Request photographs and the product model.
- Bent or unstable frame: Advise the customer to stop using the product and escalate the case.
- Broken locking mechanism: Do not recommend continued use. Escalate for inspection.
- Incorrect product received: Request the order number and photographs of the received item.

A replacement or refund must not be promised until the relevant support procedure has been reviewed.
