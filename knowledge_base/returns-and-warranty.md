
# Returns and Warranty

## Return Eligibility and Procedure

Northpeak Living accepts eligible return requests within 30 calendar days of delivery.

General return conditions:
- Products should be unused and in their original packaging.
- All included components and accessories should be returned.
- Proof of purchase or an order number is required.
- Products damaged through misuse may not qualify for a standard return.

Customers should contact Northpeak Living support to initiate a return request.

The support team reviews eligibility and provides the applicable return instructions.

Do not promise a refund or confirm return approval before the request has been reviewed.

## Warranty Coverage

Northpeak Living provides the following limited product warranties:

**Outdoor equipment**
- NF-CH1 and NF-CH2: 12 months.
- NF-TB1: 12 months.
- Eligible manufacturing defects in frames, joints, and supplied components are covered.

**Home storage**
- NS-D4, NS-W2, and NS-S2: 18 months.
- Eligible manufacturing defects in structural components and supplied hardware are covered.

The warranty period begins on the original delivery date.

Warranty coverage does not include:
- Normal wear and tear.
- Accidental damage.
- Damage caused by incorrect assembly or installation.
- Damage resulting from exceeding recommended product limits.
- Unauthorized modifications.
- Damage caused by improper storage or maintenance.

## Warranty Claims and Required Information

For a warranty assessment, collect:

1. Customer order number.
2. Product model and approximate purchase date.
3. Description of the reported defect.
4. Photographs or videos showing the issue, when available.

The support team reviews the information before deciding on the appropriate resolution.

Possible outcomes include:
- Replacement of an eligible component.
- Product replacement.
- Further inspection.
- Referral to a support colleague for manual review.

Safety-related defects and structural failures must always be escalated.

Do not guarantee warranty approval before the assessment is complete.
