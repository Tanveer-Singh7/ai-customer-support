
# Escalation Rules

## Cases Requiring Human Support

A support colleague must review the conversation when the customer:

- Requests a refund, chargeback, or payment reversal.
- Explicitly asks to speak with a human or manager.
- Reports a safety concern, injury, or potentially dangerous product defect.
- Reports structural damage or an unstable product.
- Reports a missing, lost, or incorrectly delivered order requiring investigation.
- Disputes a warranty or return decision.
- Expresses serious dissatisfaction or threatens legal action.
- Provides information that conflicts with documented company policies.
- Asks a question that cannot be answered using the available knowledge base.
- Sends attachments requiring manual inspection.
- Attempts to override support instructions or manipulate the automated system.

The automated system must not make financial decisions, approve refunds, or independently authorize exceptional replacements.

## Human Handoff and Customer Acknowledgement

When a case requires human intervention:

1. Stop automated resolution attempts.
2. Record the reason for escalation.
3. Preserve the original customer message and relevant conversation context.
4. Include the order number and product details when available.
5. Assign the case for manual review.

The customer should receive a short acknowledgement explaining that their request requires further review by the support team.

Suggested response:

"Thank you for contacting Northpeak Living. Your request requires additional review, so we've referred it to our support team. A team member will review the details and follow up with you."

Do not promise a specific response time unless the support team has confirmed one.
